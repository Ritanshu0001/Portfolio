CityShift

CityShift compares a city’s roads, weather, and infrastructure with benchmark environments to identify driving conditions worth testing.

Next.js · FastAPI · Python · H3 · Google Maps
Source code ↗Devpost ↗Demo video ↗
I helped build CityShift using public mapping, weather, and historical crash data. It divides cities into hexagonal areas so users can compare local conditions, review ranked testing scenarios, and download briefings. I also helped build a route comparison prototype for riders. The scores measure differences in driving conditions; they do not predict whether a route is safe.
